# BUG-023: Escape pressed during the 400 ms touch hold does not cancel the press: the lift still happens afterwards

- Severity: Low
- Priority: P4
- Status: Closed (Verified 2026-11-11)
- Found in: drag-drop T7 (uncommitted src/drag.js and src/app.js), QA probe 2026-11-11
- Related TC: TC-drag-drop-T7-14; requirement R18 (Escape cancels a drag; the TC extends it to an early press)
- Owner: fe

## Environment
Chromium, Pixel 7 emulation, hardware keyboard through Playwright, 4 groups.

## Steps to reproduce
1. Touch down on the row "Trả lời email" with a CDP finger and keep it still.
2. At 150 ms press Escape on the keyboard.
3. Wait 700 ms and look at html.is-dragging.

## Expected
No drag starts later, the timer is cleared and the pressing state ends (TC-drag-drop-T7-14). Blur and a hidden page do this correctly.

## Actual
The row lifts at 400 ms anyway: is-dragging, slip, strip and placeholder appear. The Escape handler returns early when the drag has not started (`if (!s.dragging ...) return`). The lift can then be cancelled by a second Escape.

## Impact
Low: needs a keyboard on a touch device and an Escape within 400 ms. R18 itself only says "during a drag", so this may be accepted by the PM and the TC narrowed.

## Evidence
- docs/project/specs/drag-drop/qa/evidence/TC-drag-drop-T7-14/
- docs/project/specs/drag-drop/qa/evidence/T7-regression/qa-probe.spec.js (the probe source)

## Suggested fix
Let Escape end a press as well (end(null) when s exists and not dragging, without stopping the event), or narrow TC-drag-drop-T7-14 to blur and hidden.

## Retest
Retest 2026-11-11 (FE fix attempt 1: Escape with a press and no drag ends the press and goes on). Pixel 7: Escape at 150 ms of the hold, wait 700 ms: no drag, no pressing class, no leftovers (blur and hidden unchanged). Mouse: Escape after a press of 3 px, then moving on: no lift, the Escape still reaches a page listener. Escape in a real drag is still swallowed (0 reached the page, the add field kept its text, one cancel announcement). Escape with no press still leaves the edit form, closes a dialog and reaches the add field. Result: Verified, Closed.

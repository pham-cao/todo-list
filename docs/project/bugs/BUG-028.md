# BUG-028: The page flip is not perceptible in real time playback

- Severity: High
- Priority: P1
- Status: Open
- Found in: page-flip (HEAD flip.js and the approved prototype); reported by the owner in two real browsers
- Related TC: TC-page-flip-T8-11 (new, real time perceptibility); missed by the paused frame cases TC-page-flip-T2-01, T2-02, T7-13
- Related requirements: R8, R1
- Owner: fe

## Environment
Chromium, real time playback with no pausing of animations, 1440 px wide. Recorded by FE with a frame capture every 16 ms. The owner saw the same in two real browsers.

## Steps to reproduce
1. Open the app on a day with one task and press the next day arrow, without pausing any animation.
2. Watch the sheet body for the 450 ms of the flip (or capture frames in real time).

## Expected
A leaf visibly turns on its left edge for most of the flip, with a moving shadow and a darker back (R1, R8).

## Actual
- The ease keeps the leaf almost still for about 200 ms (rotation under 5 degrees at 63 ms in the capture).
- perspective-origin at 100 percent collapses the leaf onto the hinge at about 67 degrees and clips the rest.
- The darker back fades in while the text still faces front.
- Only about 170 ms of the 450 ms shows a moving leaf, which reads as a flicker, not a page turn.
- The approved prototype shows the same behaviour (proto-contact.png).

## Impact
The headline feature is not perceived by the owner. All tests passed because they pause and step frames, which hides timing and easing faults.

## Evidence
- docs/project/specs/page-flip/qa/evidence/BUG-028/app-contact.png and app-log.json (app, real time)
- docs/project/specs/page-flip/qa/evidence/BUG-028/proto-contact.png and proto-log.json (prototype, real time)
- Root cause and the owner decision: docs/project/decisions.md, section "2026-11-12 page-flip motion fix"

## Fix direction (FE, agreed with the owner)
perspective-origin on the hinge, an ease that moves at once (for example cubic-bezier(0.4, 0, 0.6, 1)), the back fading in late (70 to 92 percent), --dur-flip 600 ms. The riffle becomes about 850 ms, so R3a's 700 ms budget and the lifetime helper and 700 ms waits in flip.spec.js need updating with the fix.

## Retest plan
Run TC-page-flip-T8-11 unpaused: at least 60 percent of the duration shows a leaf rotation change of at least 2 degrees per 50 ms, and the contact sheet shows a visible turn. Rerun the T8 NFR2 cases.

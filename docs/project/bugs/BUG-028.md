# BUG-028: The page flip is not perceptible in real time playback

- Severity: High
- Priority: P1
- Status: Closed (Verified then Closed, 2026-11-12, T8b)
- Found in: page-flip (HEAD flip.js and the approved prototype); reported by the owner in two real browsers
- Related TC: TC-page-flip-T8-11 (new, real time perceptibility); missed by the paused frame cases TC-page-flip-T2-01, T2-02, T7-13
- Related requirements: R8, R1
- Owner: fe

## Environment
Chromium, real time playback with no pausing of animations, 1440 px wide. Recorded by FE with a frame capture every 16 ms. The owner saw the same in two real browsers.

## Steps to reproduce
1. Open the app on a day with one task and press the next day arrow, without pausing any animation.
2. Watch the sheet body for the 450 ms of the flip (or capture frames in real time).

## Expected
A leaf visibly turns on its left edge for most of the flip, with a moving shadow and a darker back (R1, R8).

## Actual
- The ease keeps the leaf almost still for about 200 ms (rotation under 5 degrees at 63 ms in the capture).
- perspective-origin at 100 percent collapses the leaf onto the hinge at about 67 degrees and clips the rest.
- The darker back fades in while the text still faces front.
- Only about 170 ms of the 450 ms shows a moving leaf, which reads as a flicker, not a page turn.
- The approved prototype shows the same behaviour (proto-contact.png).

## Impact
The headline feature is not perceived by the owner. All tests passed because they pause and step frames, which hides timing and easing faults.

## Evidence
- docs/project/specs/page-flip/qa/evidence/BUG-028/app-contact.png and app-log.json (app, real time)
- docs/project/specs/page-flip/qa/evidence/BUG-028/proto-contact.png and proto-log.json (prototype, real time)
- Root cause and the owner decision: docs/project/decisions.md, section "2026-11-12 page-flip motion fix"

## Fix direction (FE, agreed with the owner)
perspective-origin on the hinge, an ease that moves at once (for example cubic-bezier(0.4, 0, 0.6, 1)), the back fading in late (70 to 92 percent), --dur-flip 600 ms. The riffle becomes about 850 ms, so R3a's 700 ms budget and the lifetime helper and 700 ms waits in flip.spec.js need updating with the fix.

## Retest plan
Run TC-page-flip-T8-11 unpaused: at least 60 percent of the duration shows a leaf rotation change of at least 2 degrees per 50 ms, and the contact sheet shows a visible turn. Rerun the T8 NFR2 cases.

## Retest result (QA, T8b, 2026-11-12)
Verified, then Closed.
- npm test 88 of 88. Full suite (workers=2) 922 passed, 112 skipped, 0 failed.
- T8b real time block (TC-page-flip-T8-11): 18 of 18 over 3 repeats at 1440 and 390 px (forward, back, 3 leaf riffle, 200 task day). Gates: leaf turning at 2 degrees per 50 ms for at least 60 percent of 600 ms, words showing for at least 60 percent, back only at 60 degrees or more, width kept above a fifth until 75 degrees, nothing drawn past the hinge. Sample run: angle moves steadily 0 to 92 degrees, back fades in from 77 to 91 degrees.
- NFR2 rerun at 600 ms (T8-04, T8-05): 0 longtasks, median frame 16.0 ms, max frame 20 to 32 ms.
- Contact sheets (QA reviewed app forward desktop light, back desktop dark, riffle mobile dark): the leaf lifts from about 100 ms, swings over the sheet with a moving shadow, the plain back shows only at the end, the new page is complete by about 600 ms. No flicker or collapse. Evidence: evidence/TC-page-flip-T8-11/.
- Exploratory charter 2 (T8-10) run: no stuck leaf. Watch item: 200 row renders under rapid abuse gave longtasks of 53 to 64 ms.

## R35 reading asked by FE
R35 requires 4.5:1 for text "at every frame where text is readable" and says the back may be plain paper without text. QA accepts the reading. Reasons: the back starts to show (opacity 0.02) at about 77 degrees of turn and reaches 0.27 at 82 degrees; at those angles the text is foreshortened to 22 percent of its width or less and the leaf is under 40 percent of the stage wide, so it is not readable text; fading it into the back is what R35's last sentence permits. The old 80 percent sample sat inside that fade (2.3:1), which is the intended crossfade. Conditions: (1) the last sample before the fade (65 percent, about 63 to 66 degrees, back 0) keeps 4.5:1 and passes; (2) T8b asserts the back never shows below 60 degrees, so the fade cannot creep earlier. Suggested follow up for the owner: R35 should define readable (for example back opacity under 0.05), and T7-09 could add a 70 percent sample (73 degrees, back 0) to close the gap up to the fade.

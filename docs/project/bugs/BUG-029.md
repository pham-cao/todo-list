# BUG-029: On a day of 200 wrapping rows scrolled near the bottom, the back flip copy sits 24 px below the page in full suite runs

- Severity: Medium
- Priority: P2
- Status: Closed (Verified 2026-11-13)
- Found in: page-flip T11 to T14 (uncommitted src/flip.js, tests/e2e/flip.spec.js)
- Related TC: TC-page-flip-T14-11 (existing test "T7: layout, phones and dark mode, 200 wrapping rows scrolled near the bottom at 1440 px: the copy lines up")
- Related requirements: R13, R8
- Owner: fe

## Environment
Chromium via npx playwright test, port 4173, `--workers=2`, full suite (both runs on 2026-11-13: desktop project in run 2, mobile project in run 1; the test sets its own 1440 by 900 viewport). Seed: two days of 200 wrapping rows each, scrolled near the bottom, a forward move followed by a back move.

## Steps to reproduce
1. Run the full suite: `npx playwright test --workers=2`.
2. The test at tests/e2e/flip.spec.js line 1425 fails on the back move (the forward move passes).
3. The same test alone does not fail: 16 of 16 passes at `--workers=1 --repeat-each=8` and 16 of 16 at `--workers=4 --repeat-each=8`; the whole flip.spec.js alone with `--workers=2` passes 245 of 245. It needs the load of the other specs running in parallel.

## Expected
Every row copy on the turning page sits exactly on its real row (offset 0) from the first frame, forward and back.

## Actual
Rows 191 to 199 in the window are all 24 px low (received 10562 against expected 10538 for row 191, the same 24 for every row). Diagnostics printed by the test: `back {"body":[300,276],"odd":-1,"heights":null,"fonts":"loaded","page":["24px",24,900,"852px",888]}`. The body reads 300 on the copy and 276 on the page, a 24 px difference equal to the page value 24px, so the stage is placed once with the 24 px offset and not realigned after the rows lay out. Identical numbers in both failing runs, so the offset is fixed and the occurrence depends on timing.

## Impact
For a moment during a back flip the copied rows are 24 px below the page, then the stage goes and the real rows show: a small visible jump of the paper under the curl. Limited to a very long day (200 wrapping rows) scrolled near the bottom, a back move, and a busy machine. No data is changed and no input is blocked, so Medium rather than High. FE should fix it in the next round, as the PM decided (decisions.md 2026-11-13).

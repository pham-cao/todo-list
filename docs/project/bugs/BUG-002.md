# BUG-002: Moving to a day with 200 tasks takes about 150 to 190 ms to lay out once the page has settled, over the 100 ms limit of NFR1

## Summary
- Title: Moving to a day with 200 tasks takes about 150 to 190 ms to lay out once the page has settled, over the 100 ms limit of NFR1
- Reported by: qa
- Date: 2026-11-09
- Environment: Chromium (Playwright 1.50+, headless), 1440 and 390 px, clock fixed 2026-11-09 09:00 +07, python http.server on port 4173, Linux host with load average about 2

## Preconditions
- Test data: 200 tasks dated 2026-11-08 (every third done), none on today
- Feature flags/config: none

## Steps to reproduce
1. Seed the 200 tasks and open the app on today.
2. Wait 300 ms or more after the load event.
3. In the page, take `performance.now()`, click `#prev-day`, force layout with `void document.body.offsetHeight`, take `performance.now()` again.
4. Script to run: evidence/T7-07/repro-settled-200.mjs (4 fresh pages per wait value).

## Expected result
- NFR1 / R requirement 1 under Performance: with up to 200 tasks on the day, the screen updates in under 100 ms.

## Actual result
- JavaScript takes 25 ms and the forced layout takes 130 to 190 ms, in total about 155 to 195 ms, in roughly half of the settled runs (waits of 300 ms, 1 s and 3 s). The other runs take about 40 ms. At 0 ms wait after load every run takes about 38 ms.
- The e2e case T7-07 passes because it clicks right after the load event, which lands in the fast mode. It does not see this.
- Moves to days with 0 or 1 task take 3 to 13 ms in all runs.

## Impact
- User impact: a visible hesitation of about 0.2 s on a heavy day; the day content still appears correctly.
- Scope: only days with a very large task count (200 is the stated ceiling). Caveat: headless Chromium on a loaded host with software rendering, so a real phone may differ. The result is evidence that the margin is not guaranteed, not a field measurement.

## Severity and priority
- Severity: Medium
- Priority: P3

## Evidence

- Suggested checks: find what raises the layout cost after the first moments (for example the per row write-in animation or paper background on 200 rows); consider measuring with a settled page in the e2e case.

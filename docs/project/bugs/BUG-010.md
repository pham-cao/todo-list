# BUG-010: A move with 200 tasks in 20 groups takes 250 to 300 ms once the page has settled, over the 100 ms of NFR1

- Severity: Medium
- Priority: P3
- Status: Closed (Verified 2026-11-10)
- Found in: T9 investigation of the flaky test at groups.spec.js "200 tasks in 20 groups: a move stays under 100 ms". Present since T6 (commit a1ac08f), not a regression of T9
- Related TC: TC-task-groups-T9-26 (failed), TC-task-groups-T11-16; requirement NFR1

## Environment
Chromium headless (Playwright), desktop 1440 px, clock fixed 2026-11-09 09:00 +07, python http.server, Linux host with cpufreq governor powersave and load average 2 to 3 (same family as BUG-002 of day-navigation).

## Steps to reproduce
1. Seed 200 tasks on today in 20 groups (10 each).
2. Open the app, press Sửa on the first task, choose another group in the select.
3. Wait 500 ms, then in the page take performance.now(), call requestSubmit() on the edit form, force layout with `void document.body.offsetHeight`, take performance.now().

## Expected
NFR1: with up to 200 tasks the update takes under 100 ms.

## Actual
250 to 300 ms in 7 of 10 runs (126 to 307 ms overall). Without the wait it takes 72 to 109 ms, so the old test passes most of the time and flakes about 1 run in 8. With a busy loop that keeps the CPU clocked up the move takes 70 to 93 ms, so the slow mode depends on the CPU clock. The same numbers occur at the T6 commit.
CPU profile: closeSwipes (src/app.js:611, reads scrollLeft of all 200 swipe strips on focusin) 63 to 79 ms self time, native style and layout about 190 ms.

## Impact
A visible pause of about 0.3 s when moving a task on a very heavy day on a slow or idle device. Content is correct. Caveat as in BUG-002: headless Chromium on a loaded host, so a real phone may differ.

## Evidence
- docs/project/specs/task-groups/qa/evidence/T9/speed-investigation.txt

## Suggested fix
Avoid the forced layout of 200 rows after the move: let closeSwipes skip when no swipe is open (keep the open one in `openSwipe`) instead of reading `scrollLeft` on every strip, and re-measure. Then remove `test.fixme` from the settled test. The same check should run for the add, collapse and filter timings in T11-16.

## Triage notes
Test side: the old test measured before the page settled, as day-nav did before BUG-002. It is replaced by a settled version, kept as test.fixme so the suite stays green until the fix.

## Retest
Retest 2026-11-10: closeSwipes touches only the tracked open strip. Settled move with 200 tasks in 20 groups, waits of 500 and 2000 ms, 8 runs each, desktop and mobile: 10 to 50 ms (was 126 to 307 ms). test.fixme removed, passes. Swipe regression on phones (open one, open another, tap outside, Tab into Sửa, delete note from a just swiped strip, peek hint) passes: existing app.spec, day-nav and groups tests plus a new test, 3 runs. Evidence: speed-retest.txt, swipe-regression.txt. Result: Verified, Closed.

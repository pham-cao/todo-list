# BUG-018: A long press on a row that sits under the drop strip, lifted without moving, silently moves the task into the group under the finger

- Severity: Medium
- Priority: P3
- Status: Closed (Verified 2026-11-11)
- Found in: drag-drop T5 (uncommitted src/drag.js touch path), edge probe on Pixel 7
- Related TC: TC-drag-drop-T5-15 (long press then lift without moving is a cancel); requirements R22, R9
- Owner: fe

## Environment
Chromium, Playwright "Pixel 7" (412 x 839, touch via CDP), same result at 360 x 740 and 390 x 844. Clock fixed 2026-11-09 09:00 +07, vi-VN, npm start on port 4173. 3 groups, 4 tasks, "Cắt tóc" ungrouped and the last row.

## Steps to reproduce
1. Seed the 4 tasks and 3 groups of the T5 tests. Scroll to the bottom, so that the last row "Cắt tóc" sits in the bottom band of the screen (y about 756 of 839; the page cannot scroll further).
2. Touch the row text and hold still for 450 ms. The strip slides in over the bottom of the screen and the finger is now on the label "Nhà".
3. Lift the finger without moving.

## Expected
The same as a long press on any other row lifted in place (TC-drag-drop-T5-15): a cancel, "Đã hủy. Việc vẫn ở nhóm ...", nothing written.

## Actual
The label "Nhà" gets `drop-target--over` as soon as the strip appears, and the lift drops the task into "Nhà": one write to the tasks key, `groupId` is "nha", announced as a move. The user never moved the finger and never saw the strip before the drop.

## Impact
Silent change of data from a plain long press on the last rows of a short list, which on a phone are often under the strip band. Only a second long press and drag undoes it. Not data loss. The T4 decision says the fixed strip covering lower rows is correct, but it does not cover a release without any move.

## Suggested fix
Ignore the hit test until the finger has moved at least a few pixels after the pick up (or until the strip's entry has finished), so a lift in place is always a cancel. Alternative: accept as is and document in decisions.md.

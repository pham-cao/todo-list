# BUG-001: Về hôm nay tab text flashes faint for about 170 ms when opening a non today address

## Summary
- Title: Về hôm nay tab text flashes faint for about 170 ms when opening a non today address
- Reported by: qa
- Date: 2026-11-09
- Environment: Chromium (Playwright), 1440 and 390 px, light and dark, clock fixed 2026-11-09 09:00 +07, python http.server on port 4173

## Preconditions
- Test data: any task data
- Feature flags/config: none

## Steps to reproduce
1. Open the app at `/#2026-11-08` (reload or paste the address).
2. Watch the Về hôm nay tab during the first 200 ms, or sample its computed colors on every frame.

## Expected result
- The tab appears filled with readable text from the first frame.

## Actual result
- The page paints with the muted today state, then `aria-disabled` is removed and the 140 ms `background-color` transition runs. The text color switches at once to the on-pen color while the background is still transparent, so about 10 frames show white text (light) or dark navy text (dark) on the paper. Samples: light 1440 frames from 18 ms to 176 ms; dark 1440 frames up to 178 ms.

## Impact
- User impact: a brief flicker of an almost invisible label on every load of a past or future address. Content remains reachable and settles quickly.
- Scope: all viewports and both color schemes. Frequency: always.

## Severity and priority
- Severity: Low (Trivial cosmetic)
- Priority: P3, fix in T7

## Evidence
- Evidence: docs/project/specs/day-navigation/qa/evidence/T6-all/bug-001-samples.txt, evidence/T6-tab-load-1440-light.png, evidence/T6-tab-load-390-light.png (settled state)
- Suggested fix: set the state on the element before first paint (apply `aria-disabled` removal in the initial render before styles apply, or disable the transition on first render, or transition `color` together with the background).

- Status: Closed (Verified then Closed 2026-11-09, T7 retest)

Task groups

Prototype on the paper notebook design, version 3

A clickable prototype for putting tasks into the owner's own groups, such as Công ty, Nhà and Học tiếng Nhật. Each group is a highlighted heading in the day's list, sections fold away, one group can be shown on its own, and a small index card manages names, colours, deleting and moving all tasks of a group. All interface text is Vietnamese, word for word from the requirements, and dark mode follows your system setting.

Start here

Open a day before any group exists, choose Nhóm mới... and follow the links to create Gia đình and add a task to it. Then open a day with groups and try folding, the filter, Sửa and Quản lý nhóm. The flows, screen inventory and requirement coverage are in flows.md, the new components in design-system.md (section "Task groups"), and the review notes in critique.md.

Key design decisions

  • Group labels are torn paper labels, like a label torn off a strip: coloured paper with the grain, both ends torn into a sawtooth (răng cưa), a small tilt (alternating per section) and a soft shadow that follows the torn edges. A section header is one ruled line: the fold chevron in the margin where the checkboxes are, the torn label with the group name, and the done and total count at the end. A blank line separates sections, as on paper. Chưa phân nhóm is a blank white label torn the same way, with pencil dashes along its edges, so it still reads as a group. The same label appears everywhere a group shows: as a mini label in the add, edit, filter and move choices, as the name in Quản lý nhóm rows, as the colour choices, and stuck on the corner of the delete and move notes. (Revised twice at the owner's request: first highlighter, then sticker, now torn label.)
  • Colour is never the only cue: the name is always written, mini labels carry a thin darker outline so they stay visible on paper, and the chosen colour in the dialog is lifted, framed in ink and ticked. Black text on the light labels is 13.5:1 or better, warm white on the deep night labels is 7.4:1 or better, and muted ink on the blank label is 7.5:1 (light) and 5.7:1 (dark).
  • The group choice lives on the add line on desktop and on a second line on phones, so a group can be picked or created without leaving the add flow. Nhóm mới... opens one extra line with the name and Tạo nhóm, and the margin previews the colour the new group will get.
  • One group bar sits directly above the list: Lọc theo nhóm on the left (from the first group on) and Quản lý nhóm on the right (always, on every day). When a filter is on, the funnel is inked in so the owner knows part of the day is hidden.
  • Quản lý nhóm opens an index card rather than the sticky note, because it is a small form and needs a straighter, calmer surface. Questions that need an answer (delete, delete blocked) keep the approved sticky note, stacked on top of the card.
  • A day where no task has a group looks exactly as it does today: no headers, the same rows, swipe and stamp.
